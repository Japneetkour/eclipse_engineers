======================================================================================================================
FINAL ANSWER
======================================================================================================================
1. For a fresher: your pay climbs fastest in your first few years - about +36% a year up to 6.5 years of experience (95% CI 6.0-7.0 years), then only about +7% a year after that, so the big raises happen early.
2. In rupees the median salary jumps from 1.5L (0-3 years) to 8L (4-5 years) to 12.5L (6-9 years) - about 8x while you are junior - then only crawls from 12.5L to 20L across 10-20 years, so a fresher wins most of the pay growth in the first ~6 years.
3. Top earners do NOT flatten at the same point as the median: the median's curve breaks at 5.0 years (all-postings fit: 6.5 years) while the 90th percentile keeps rising until 10.0 years before flattening near 0% a year - but that flattening is partly an artefact, because 26% of 10+ year postings already sit in the top 25-50L band, so pay above it is never recorded.
